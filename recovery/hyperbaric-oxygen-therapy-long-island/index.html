Frequently Asked Questions

ATA means atmospheres absolute. A 1.6 ATA session raises chamber pressure to 1.6 times normal atmospheric pressure.

Most people notice pressure in the ears, similar to takeoff or landing in an airplane. The Apex team explains equalization techniques and increases pressure gradually.

A typical session lasts about 60–90 minutes, including compression and decompression.

Dry Dive Training uses the chamber's controlled pressure to help divers practice equalization, build confidence, and prepare for depth without entering the water.

No. Dry Dive Training is a supplemental pressure-adaptation experience and does not replace formal scuba instruction, certification, or in-water training.

Ask your doctor whether supervised 1.6 ATA hyperbaric sessions are appropriate if you have a medical condition, are receiving treatment, are pregnant, or have concerns about pressure exposure.
