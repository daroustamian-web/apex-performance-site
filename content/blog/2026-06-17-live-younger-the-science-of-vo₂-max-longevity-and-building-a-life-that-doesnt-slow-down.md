## What Is VO₂ Max?

VO₂ Max is the maximum amount of oxygen your body can utilize during exercise.

Think of it as the size of your engine.

A larger engine means:

* More energy
* Better endurance
* Better cardiovascular health
* Better recovery
* Better performance
* Greater resilience against disease

Most importantly...

**Higher VO₂ Max is one of the strongest predictors of longevity ever discovered.**

Not supplements.

Not biohacks.

Not expensive gadgets.

Your body's ability to take in oxygen, transport it, and use it efficiently may tell us more about your future health than almost any other metric.
